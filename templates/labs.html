Command Labs

Every lab has four parts: what the command is for, how it works, a simulated read-and-interpret exercise, and a real command you run on your own machine and paste the output from. The backend never executes anything.

Why paste output? Reading real output is the actual skill. The grader checks your paste against curated patterns — presence of the right fields, not cosmetic formatting. Your pasted text is never stored, only the rubric result.

How a lab is scored

The simulated questions are graded as multiple choice. The real exercise is graded on your pasted output against expected patterns: if all the required evidence is present, the lab counts as practised. Mastery for a lab is half simulated, half real — which is why skipping the real half caps a module at 100% only in theory, and 87% in practice.
