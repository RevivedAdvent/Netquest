Troubleshooting Case Studies

Each case gives you symptoms and a toolbox. You collect evidence command by command, state a hypothesis, commit to a diagnosis, and then choose the fix. Partial credit comes from breadth of evidence; passing comes from the correct root cause.

How it works: some evidence requires other evidence first — you cannot sensibly check a return route before you have seen the forward path fail. The grader rejects diagnoses that blame the wrong layer, and it penalises fixes that only mask the symptom.
